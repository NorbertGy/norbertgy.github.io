Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-20

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Cat vs Glass (“the App”), developed and published by Norbert Győri, an independent developer operating in Denmark (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

1. Overview

Cat vs Glass is a casual mobile game. The App does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide their name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, health data, or other directly identifying personal information to play.

The App uses third-party services for advertising, consent management, analytics, crash reporting, diagnostics, notifications, and in-app purchases. These services may process limited data as described below.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores gameplay and preference data locally on your device, including:

  • the current game timer and run state,
  • best scores and local game progress,
  • sound, haptics, and reminder settings,
  • selected and locally unlocked skin packs,
  • a local purchase ownership cache after a successful purchase or restore,
  • local reward-double and ad-frequency limits,
  • local review-prompt state.

This local data is used to provide App functionality and is not sent to our own server. Cat vs Glass does not currently operate a custom backend account system or developer-controlled user database.

3. Notifications

The App may offer optional local reminder notifications before the current game timer reaches zero. Notification permission is requested only when you enable this feature. Reminder preferences and scheduled notification details are stored locally on your device. The App does not use these reminders to send marketing messages from a remote server.

4. Advertising

The App may show ads using Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ads may include optional rewarded ads, such as watching a video to double a game reward, and limited interstitial ads at natural game breaks.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share data such as app interactions, approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, diagnostics, and related ad performance information for purposes including advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use Google services: Google partner sites policy.

5. Consent Management

Where required, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage privacy choices for advertising and related storage. Your choices may affect whether personalized or non-personalized ads are shown and whether certain advertising or analytics storage is enabled.

You can review or change available choices in the App under Settings → Manage privacy choices, where available.

6. In-App Purchases

Cat vs Glass offers optional one-time purchases, such as cosmetic skin packs and Remove Ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

The App stores a local ownership cache so purchased products can be unlocked after purchase or restore. Google Play remains the source for payment processing and purchase restoration.

7. Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand how the App is used, improve gameplay balance, measure feature performance, and evaluate rewarded-ad, purchase, and skin-selection flows.

Firebase Analytics may process information such as app opens, minigame starts and finishes, scores, earned reward minutes, rewarded-ad interactions, purchase-flow events, selected skins, approximate location, device information, app-instance identifiers, and advertising identifiers where available and permitted.

We do not use Firebase Analytics to collect your name, email address, account login details, contacts, photos, messages, or precise location.

8. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

The App uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to detect, diagnose, prioritize, and fix crashes, non-fatal errors, ANRs, and other technical issues.

Firebase Crashlytics may process crash logs, stack traces, diagnostic information, device metadata, App state at the time of a crash, technical breadcrumbs, and crash-related identifiers. This information is used to improve App stability and fix bugs.

9. Third-Party Service Providers

The App uses services provided by third parties, including:

  • Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads,
  • Google User Messaging Platform,
  • Google Play Billing,
  • Google Firebase Analytics,
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and terms. Their practices may vary based on your region, device settings, consent choices, and the services enabled in the App.

10. Data Security

Data transmitted to third-party services is generally protected in transit using industry-standard encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS. We take reasonable steps to limit the data used by the App, but no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay and preference data remains on your device until it is overwritten, reset, cleared through device settings, or removed by uninstalling the App. We do not retain this local data on our own servers.

Analytics, advertising, purchase, consent, and crash information processed by Google is retained according to Google’s applicable policies, controls, and retention settings. Because this information is not connected to a Cat vs Glass account and we do not receive information that directly identifies you, we may be unable to locate or delete records associated with a specific individual.

You can manage available privacy choices in the App under Settings → Manage privacy choices. You may also reset or delete device identifiers, such as the advertising ID, through your device settings where available. Purchase records are managed by Google Play.

12. Children

Cat vs Glass is not directed to children. The current intended target audience is users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect directly identifying personal information from children through the App.

13. International Processing

Third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and applicable legal safeguards.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

15. Contact
