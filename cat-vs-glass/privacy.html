Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-26

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Cat vs Glass (“the App”), developed by Norbert Győri (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

1. Overview

Cat vs Glass is a casual mobile game. The App does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide their name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, health data, or other directly identifying personal information to play.

The App may use third-party services for advertising, consent management, analytics, crash reporting, diagnostics, and in-app purchases. These services may process limited data as described below.

2. Data We Store Locally on Your Device

The App stores gameplay and preference data locally on your device, such as:

  • current game timer and run state,
  • best survival time and local game progress,
  • sound and haptics settings,
  • selected skin pack,
  • local purchase ownership cache after successful purchase or restore,
  • local reward-double and ad-frequency limits.

This local data is used for app functionality and is not sent to our own server. Cat vs Glass does not currently operate a custom backend account system.

3. Advertising

The App may show ads using Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ads may include optional rewarded ads, such as watching a video to double a game reward, and limited interstitial ads at natural game breaks.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share data such as app interactions, approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, diagnostics, and related ad performance information for purposes including advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Depending on your region, you may be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown or before certain advertising and analytics storage is enabled.

You can learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use Google services here: Google partner sites policy.

4. Consent Management

For users in regions where consent is required, the App may use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage consent choices for ads and analytics. Your choices may affect whether personalized or non-personalized ads are shown and whether analytics storage is enabled.

You can review or change available privacy choices in the App under Settings → Manage privacy choices, where available.

5. In-App Purchases

Cat vs Glass may offer optional one-time purchases, such as cosmetic skin packs and Remove Ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

The App may store a local ownership cache so purchased products can be unlocked immediately after purchase or restore. Google Play remains the source for purchase and restore flows.

6. Analytics

The App may use Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand how the App is used, improve gameplay balance, measure feature performance, and evaluate rewarded ad, purchase, and skin-selection flows.

Firebase Analytics may collect app usage information such as app opens, minigame starts and finishes, scores, earned reward minutes, rewarded ad interactions, purchase flow events, selected skins, approximate location, device information, app-instance identifiers, and advertising identifiers where available and permitted.

We do not use Firebase Analytics to collect your name, email address, account login details, contacts, photos, messages, or precise location.

7. Crash Reporting and Diagnostics

The App may use Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google, to detect, diagnose, prioritize, and fix crashes, non-fatal errors, ANRs, and technical issues.

Firebase Crashlytics may collect crash logs, stack traces, diagnostic information, device metadata, app state at the time of a crash, breadcrumbs or technical logs, and crash-related identifiers. This information is used to improve app stability and fix bugs.

8. Children

Cat vs Glass is not directed to children. The current intended target audience is users aged 18 and over.

9. Data Deletion

Because the App does not require an account and does not store gameplay data on our own servers, most app data can be deleted by uninstalling the App or clearing the App’s local storage on your device.

Cat vs Glass does not require or provide user accounts, and we do not collect directly identifying information such as your name or email address. Because analytics and crash data are not directly linked to an account or identity that we can access, we may not be able to identify and delete data related to a specific individual user.

You can manage available privacy choices in the App under Settings → Manage privacy choices. You may also reset or delete certain device identifiers, such as the advertising ID, through your device settings where available.

Purchase records are managed by Google Play and are subject to Google Play’s own policies and account controls.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

11. Contact
