Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-25

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Cat vs Glass (“the App”), developed by Norbert Győri (“we”, “us”, or “our”).

1. Overview

Cat vs Glass is a casual mobile game. The App does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide their name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, health data, or other directly identifying personal information to play.

The App may use third-party services for advertising, consent management, and in-app purchases. These services may process limited data as described below.

2. Data We Store Locally on Your Device

The App stores gameplay and preference data locally on your device, such as:

  • current game timer and run state,
  • best survival time and local game progress,
  • sound and haptics settings,
  • selected skin pack,
  • local purchase ownership cache after successful purchase or restore,
  • local reward-double and ad-frequency limits.

This local data is used for app functionality and is not sent to our own server. Cat vs Glass does not currently operate a custom backend account system.

3. Advertising

The App may show ads using Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK. Ads may include optional rewarded ads, such as watching a video to double a game reward, and limited interstitial ads at natural game breaks.

Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share data such as app interactions, device or advertising identifiers, and related ad performance information for purposes including advertising, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Depending on your region, you may be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

You can learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use Google services here: Google partner sites policy.

4. Consent Management

For users in regions where consent is required, the App may use Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent. Your choices may affect whether personalized or non-personalized ads are shown.

5. In-App Purchases

Cat vs Glass may offer optional one-time purchases, such as cosmetic skin packs and Remove Ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card information.

The App may store a local ownership cache so purchased products can be unlocked immediately after purchase or restore. Google Play remains the source for purchase and restore flows.

6. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Cat vs Glass does not currently use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. If analytics or crash reporting services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated accordingly.

7. Children

Cat vs Glass is not directed to children. The current intended target audience is users aged 18 and over.

8. Data Deletion

Because the App does not require an account and does not store gameplay data on our own servers, most app data can be deleted by uninstalling the App or clearing the App’s local storage on your device.

Purchase records are managed by Google Play and are subject to Google Play’s own policies and account controls.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: noisenest.app@gmail.com
